The Methodology

Training Disciplines

Four periodized pathways engineered by collegiate strength coaches. Every program includes precise load prescriptions and velocity profiling.

01 Barbell Mastery

STRENGTH

Build uncompromising raw power through structured progressive barbell, trap-bar, and Olympic movements. Focus on neural drive, rate of force development (RFD), and structural joint resilience.

EQUIPMENT: Eleiko IPF Calibrated Plates
BENCHMARK: 2.5x BW Squat / 3x Deadlift
INTENSITY: RPE 8.0 – 9.5
02 Physique Architecture

HYPERTROPHY

Biomechanically optimized muscle development without joint destruction. Leverage custom resistance curves, lengthened partial overloads, and high-precision converging/diverging machines.

EQUIPMENT: Arsenal & Prime Fitness Line
FOCUS: Mechanical Tension & Overload
INTENSITY: 0 – 2 RIR (In Reserve)
03 Metabolic Capacity

CONDITIONING

Develop a gas tank that outlasts the competition. High-output sprint intervals on curved treadmills, heavy sled turf drags, Concept2 erg fleets, and lactate threshold testing.

EQUIPMENT: Woodway Curve, SkiErg, Echo Bikes
ZONE TARGET: Zone 2 Base & Zone 5 Sprint
ENERGY SYSTEM: Phosphagen & Glycolytic Capacity
04 1-on-1 Periodization

PERSONAL COACHING

Dedicated bespoke execution. From blood work interpretation and body composition scans to weekly biomechanics adjustments and customized macro nutrient programming.

ACCESS: Dedicated CSCS Senior Coach
CHECK-IN: Weekly Video & VBT Profiling
NUTRITION: Micronutrient Periodization
